

1. Pitch Deck 
	- In Crisp Dreams: Dream Brewing
	- Craft your dreams into reality by brewing essential elements of life
	- Players enter a serene yet vibrant world where they take on the role of a "Dream Brewer."
2. Synopsis 
	- Storyline:
	- Players enter a surreal world, where they are guided by a Guardian NPC. The mission: collect the essential elements of life—a magical seed, a can of sunshine, a handful of soil, and a drop of pure water.
	- As player through abstract dreamscapes, player meet the Ancestor Deer, a mystical figure who shares profound insights about growth, balance, and connection.
	- Crisp Dreams conveys a powerful message: that nothing is needed more than the physical essentials of life. The game encourages players to let go of unnecessary stress and overanalysis.
